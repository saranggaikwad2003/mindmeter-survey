About this project

MCA research prototype on smartphone-use patterns among college students.

Features

  • 15 survey questions and optional comment
  • Consent prompt for adults aged 18+
  • Central SQLite server database
  • Admin login, dashboard and CSV export
  • Survey link copy and WhatsApp sharing

Privacy and limitations

No names, email addresses, phone numbers or student IDs are requested. The indicator score is a simple exploratory rule, not a validated clinical scale or medical diagnosis. Obtain supervisor approval before collecting real research responses.
